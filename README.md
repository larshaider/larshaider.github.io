# larshaider.github.io

Personal site and legal pages for my apps, served by GitHub Pages from `main` (no build step).

- `/` – profile and app list (neutral theme, follows system light/dark: `style.css`)
- `/yodo/` – You Only Die Once: app page with screenshots and Play Store link (app theme: `yodo/yodo.css` on top of `style.css`)
- `/yodo/privacy/` – privacy policy (English)
- `/yodo/privacy/de/` – Datenschutzerklärung (German)

Keep both privacy languages in sync and update the effective date on every change.

Screenshots in `yodo/img/` are the raw phone shots from the yodo repo (`store/raw/phone`), scaled to 540 px wide WebP. Cinzel font under the SIL Open Font License (`yodo/fonts/OFL.txt`).
