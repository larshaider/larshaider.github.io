You Only Die Once

Boss tracker for the Souls games

An Android app to track which bosses and mini bosses you have killed in the Souls games.

Features

  • Demon's Souls, Dark Souls, Dark Souls II, Dark Souls III and Bloodborne
  • Every boss and mini boss, grouped by area
  • Soul points: 5 per boss, 3 per mini boss
  • Works offline, no account, no ads, no tracking
